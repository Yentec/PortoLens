# 0005. Money, currencies, dates and identifiers

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-07

## Decision

### Money

- **One sign convention**: amounts are signed from the account's point of view (positive = money
  in). No separate direction column.
- Storage: `NUMERIC(19,4)`; scale per currency enforced by the domain (EUR = 2 decimals).
- Domain: a `Money` value object (amount + currency) built on decimal.js; arithmetic across
  currencies throws. Never floating point.
- API: amounts are decimal **strings**; responses use `{ "amount": "-93.84", "currency": "EUR" }`.

### Currencies

- The currency belongs to the account and is immutable; entries carry no currency column.
- Prototype: EUR only (database `CHECK`), workspace `baseCurrency = EUR`.
- Later: an `ExchangeRate` table (ECB reference rates); flows at their historical amount, balances
  converted at the reporting date; converted amounts are never stored as truth. A multi-currency
  brokerage is one single-currency account per currency, grouped by the investment module.

### Dates

- **Business dates** (entry, tracking start, checkpoint) are calendar dates: PostgreSQL `DATE`, no
  time, no time zone. **Instants** (`createdAt`, audit) are `timestamptz` in UTC and converted to
  local time by the frontend.
- A `CalendarDate` type in the domain; `YYYY-MM-DD` strings at every boundary. Prisma `Date` values
  are converted with `getUTC*` only. The frontend never formats business dates through a local
  `Date`.
- "Today" is computed in `Workspace.timezone` (default `Europe/Paris`).
- Future-dated operations are forbidden in the prototype. Planned operations will be a distinct
  status (`PLANNED`) with recurring operations, shown up to one month ahead.

### Identifiers

- UUIDv7 generated by the application (time-ordered, created before persistence).

## Consequences

- Monthly aggregation never depends on a time zone or daylight saving time.
- Lifting EUR-only means dropping one `CHECK` and adding a per-currency scale rule.
